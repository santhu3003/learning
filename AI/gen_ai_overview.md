# What is Gen AI?

**Generative AI (Gen AI)** is a type of artificial intelligence that can create new content by learning patterns from huge amounts of data.

Think of it as AI that can write, draw, compose, design, or generate things instead of just giving yes/no answers.

---

## 💡 Simple Definition

> **Gen AI is AI that can produce new content like text, images, audio, code, or video using what it learned from data.**

## 📝 Examples of Generative AI in Action

- Writing essays, emails, or summaries
- Generating realistic images from text prompts

- Generating code or fixing code errors
- Translating and simplifying text

# What are NLPs and LLMs?

## 🔡 NLP (Natural Language Processing)

NLP is a field of AI that helps computers understand, interpret, and use human language.

**Examples of NLP:**
- Language translation (like Google Translate)
- Sentiment analysis (detecting positive/negative tone)
- Speech-to-text (like voice typing)
- Chatbots

> **Basically:** NLP is how computers understand language.

---

## 🤖 LLMs (Large Language Models)

LLMs are a type of AI model trained on massive amounts of text so they can:
- Understand language
- Generate text
- Answer questions
- Hold conversations
- Reason based on information

**Examples of LLMs:**
- GPT models (ChatGPT)
- Gemini models
- Claude models
- LLaMA models

---

# What is RLHF?

**RLHF** stands for **Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback**.

It’s a method used to train AI models (like ChatGPT) to behave more helpfully, safely, and in a more human-aligned way.
